# TechNova Laptops - Refund & Return Policy

## Return window
- Standard laptops (AlphaBook, GammaAir, SpectraBook, NanoEdge): returns accepted within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Gaming laptops (OmegaPro series): returns accepted within 15 days of the delivery date, because of high-wear components.

## Condition
- The laptop must include the original charger, box and accessories.
- Laptops with physical damage caused by the customer (cracked screen, liquid damage) are not eligible for a refund, but can be sent for paid repair.

## Orders not yet delivered
- An order that has not been delivered yet can be cancelled at no cost for a full refund.

## Refund method and timing
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned laptop is received and inspected.

## Restocking fee
- No restocking fee for unopened laptops.
- A 10% restocking fee applies to opened laptops returned for reasons other than a defect.

## Defective products
- Laptops that arrive defective (dead on arrival, faulty hardware) get a full refund or free replacement with no restocking fee, at any point inside the return window.

## Shipping costs
- Return shipping is free for defective items. For all other returns, a $25 return shipping label is deducted from the refund.
